🌱 LingoSprout — Support

Need help with LingoSprout, found a bug, or want to suggest a feature? Open an issue on our issue tracker and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

💬 Open a support request

A free GitHub account is needed to post, and requests there are publicly visible, so please don't include personal details in one. If your question concerns privacy or your own data, you can ask it without identifying yourself or your child — see our Privacy Policy, which explains why we hold nothing about either of you in the first place.

Frequently asked questions

Does LingoSprout need the internet?
No. LingoSprout works completely offline.
Does it collect any data about my child?
No. Nothing leaves your device. See our Privacy Policy.
Can I set up more than one child?
Yes. LingoSprout supports multiple profiles, so siblings (or a whole classroom) can each have their own vault of characters.
How does my child learn to write a character?
LingoSprout animates the correct stroke order, then lets your child trace the character in a guided writing square.
How do I delete our data?
All data is stored on the device. Delete a profile inside the app to remove that profile and its vault, or delete the app to remove everything. No copy exists anywhere else.
