🌱 LingoSprout — Age Suitability

Last updated: 19 August 2026

LingoSprout teaches the writing, pronunciation, and meaning of Chinese characters. It is rated 4+ because it contains nothing objectionable at any age, and it is designed to be useful to learners from about 5 through to 18, and to adults beginning Chinese.

Why the range is wide

Chinese characters are learned the same way at every age: you learn what a character means, how it sounds, and the stroke order for writing it. A beginner is a beginner whether they are five or fifty. LingoSprout is built around that single task rather than around a particular school year, so the same tools serve a child starting their first characters and a secondary student revising for an exam.

Each learner keeps their own vault of characters and adds to it at their own pace. There is no fixed curriculum, no age-graded levels, and no time pressure, so the app naturally follows whatever level the learner is at.

Younger learners

Children in early primary school can use LingoSprout comfortably, typically alongside a parent or teacher who helps them choose characters to add. The interface uses large touch targets, simple navigation, and minimal reading. Tracing a character with a finger needs no reading ability at all.

Older learners

Secondary students and adults use the same features independently: pinyin search across a full offline dictionary, stroke-order reference for characters they have not met before, and self-graded quizzing for revision. Nothing in the app is locked behind an age setting.

What the app does not contain

There is nothing in LingoSprout that would make it unsuitable for a young child, or that a parent would need to supervise:

  • No advertising of any kind.
  • No in-app purchases or subscriptions.
  • No links out of the app, and no web browsing.
  • No chat, messaging, sharing, or any other contact between users.
  • No user-generated content that anyone else can see.
  • No internet connection — the app works entirely offline.
  • No data collection or tracking. See our Privacy Policy.
