🌱 LingoSprout — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

LingoSprout is a Chinese-character learning app for learners of all ages, from early primary school upwards. We built it with privacy first. This policy explains, in plain language, what LingoSprout does and does not do with information.

The short version

LingoSprout does not collect any personal information. The app works entirely offline on your device. There are no accounts, no logins, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. Nothing you or your child enters ever leaves the device.

Information we collect

None. LingoSprout does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. We have no servers.

Information stored on your device

To make the app work, LingoSprout saves things like the profile name, chosen avatar, and the list of characters your child is learning. This information is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.

You can remove it at any time: delete a child's profile inside the app to remove that profile and everything it learned, or delete the app to remove all of it. No copy exists anywhere else, so nothing further is needed.

Sound

When LingoSprout speaks a character aloud, it uses the Mandarin text-to-speech voice built into iOS, which runs on your device. No recording is made, no microphone is used, and no audio is sent anywhere.

Children's privacy

LingoSprout is used by children as well as older learners. Because the app collects no personal information and includes no advertising, tracking, in-app purchases, or external links, it is consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the child-protection provisions of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Australian Privacy Act.

Parents sometimes ask how to review or delete the information an app holds about their child. In LingoSprout there is nothing for us to show or delete, because we never receive anything — a child's name and avatar are simply labels saved on your own device, under your control, and removable as described above.

Third parties

LingoSprout contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or software development kits (SDKs). We do not share data with anyone because we do not collect any.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
